PromptCAD — Underware Generator

This page renders 3D-printable Underware 2.0 parts from a URL. It's meant to be driven by an AI agent, not opened directly — give the agent the skill below and describe the part you want.

  1. Download the skill and give it to your agent (Claude, ChatGPT, or anything with browser access).
  2. Describe the part in plain language — e.g. "a 2-unit cable channel, 24mm tall, snap connector."
  3. The agent encodes the part as a URL and navigates here for you.
  4. Preview it in 3D, then download the STL.
